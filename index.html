About

I began exploring code while studying for the MCAT and customizing Anki cards, then continued building through self-directed projects focused on learning, accessibility, communication, and everyday problem-solving. My background in the life sciences shaped the way I approach product work: with empathy, precision, and a strong bias toward tools that make complex tasks feel more manageable.

I have also developed a strong interest in AI-assisted workflows and prompt-based problem solving, using tools such as Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot to accelerate research, testing, iteration, and learning. At the same time, I care deeply about understanding the underlying code itself, and I am intentionally building the judgment to balance AI leverage with real technical fluency. I am especially interested in QA testing, health-tech, accessibility-minded product work, and front-end experiences that feel thoughtful, useful, and human.
